Enter the archive

Sign in to sync your ledger, or open a local copy stored in this browser.

What creating an account stores: your email and password are held by Supabase Auth for sign-in. Your watched/unwatched status, star rating, priority-queue flag, and any notes you write are stored in our Supabase database, tied to your account, and sync across your devices. Poster images are cached in shared cloud storage (not tied to your account) so they load faster for everyone. Nothing else leaves this browser: any TMDB/OMDb API keys you add in Settings, your search/filter choices, and your AI recommendation provider settings stay only on this device. You can export everything above at any time from Settings → Archive copies.

Local data stays in this browser and is not password protected. Export a copy from Settings for safekeeping.
